Study 2: Robot Workholding
Conditions: Freedrive, AR Handle, Hybrid

Workload
1. Mental Demand — How mentally demanding was moving the board this way?
2. Physical Demand — How physically demanding was moving the board this way?
3. Temporal Demand — How hurried or rushed did this method feel?
4. Performance — How successful do you feel you were at positioning the board correctly?
5. Effort — How hard did you have to work to position the board this way?
6. Frustration — How insecure, discouraged, irritated, stressed, or annoyed did you feel using this method?
Controllability
7. Fine Adjustment — How simple were small adjustments (tasks 6–10)?
8. Coarse Adjustment — How simple were large adjustments (tasks 1–5)?
9. Naturalness — How natural did it feel to control the robot’s movement this way?
Predictability
10. Movement — How easy was it to predict how the robot was going to move?
11. Position — How easy was it to predict where the robot was going to move?
12. Onset Timing — How clearly could you tell when the robot was going to start moving?
Safety
13. Movement — How safe did you feel while the robot was moving?
14. Stationary — How safe did you feel that the robot was not going to move unexpectedly?
Trust
15. Trust — How much did you trust this method to safely position the board?
Other
16. Additional Comments — Any additional comments?
17. Overall Preference — Rank Freedrive, AR Handle, and Hybrid from most preferred (1) to least preferred (3).


Study 4: Part Request
Conditions: Gesture, Language+Gesture, Task-Aware
Workload
1. Mental Demand — How mentally demanding was it to request the part this way?
2. Physical Demand — How physically demanding was it to request the part this way?
3. Temporal Demand — How hurried or rushed did requesting the part feel?
4. Performance — How successful do you feel you were at getting the correct part?
5. Effort — How hard did you have to work to get the correct part?
6. Frustration — How insecure, discouraged, irritated, stressed, or annoyed did you feel while requesting the part?
Confidence
7. System — How confident were you that the system understood what you needed?
8. Retrieval — How confident were you that the system was going to bring you the correct part?
Usability
9. Part Detection — How clear was it which part you needed to request?
10. Part Selection — How easy was it to request the part that you wanted?
Adaptability
11. Phrasing — How much did you need to tailor the phrasing of your request to the system?
12. Movement — How much did you need to adjust your position to request the part?
Other
13. Additional Comments — Any additional comments?
14. Overall Preference — Rank Gesture, Language+Gesture, and Task-Aware from most preferred (1) to least preferred (3).
