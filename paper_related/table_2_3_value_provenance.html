One Experiment 3 procedure/count distinction requires clarification, described below.

How internally calculated values are produced

  • Mean and SD: trials/steps are first summarized within participant and condition; the table reports the mean and sample SD across participants.
  • Friedman test: three paired condition vectors are compared; Table II objective tests use N=10 and questionnaire tests use N=11.
  • Kendall’s W: Friedman chi-square / [N × (3−1)].
  • Pairwise prose values: paired, two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests followed by Holm correction across the three condition pairs for each metric.

Grounding-issue percentages

These are calculated from study4_conversations.csv, not from study4_condition_summary.csv:

  • Language 3.9%: 9 issue turns / 232 total Language turns = 3.879%.
  • Task-Aware 5.2%: 12 issue turns / 231 total Task-Aware turns = 5.195%.
  • Task-Aware D3-specific 24.7%: 57 task-state query/cycle turns / 231 total Task-Aware turns = 24.675%. The 57 comprise selected_step_physical_cycle (33), selected_step_needs_highlight (18), selected_step_object_already_acquired (4), and step_cycle_empty (2).

study4_conversation_review.py exports non-clean turns for inspection, but the 3.9% and 5.2% paper values follow the decision-category grouping above. The 0% wrong-selection values are structurally different: selection is logged only after language grounding resolves.

Procedure/count clarification needed. The paper says participants completed “the same seven matched partial-assembly steps.” The raw Study 4 configuration in study4_full_report.py contains eight step IDs: six bearing/gear-rod steps and two fastening instances. The script collapses the two fastening instances into one reporting group for per-step summaries, yielding seven groups, but the Table III per-session totals include the raw completed steps (generally eight; some sessions contain seven). Therefore, describe this as “eight task instances grouped into seven step types,” or recalculate the totals using exactly seven instances.
