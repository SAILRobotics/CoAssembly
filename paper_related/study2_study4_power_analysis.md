# Study 2 and Study 4 Power Analysis

Three-condition Friedman tests; alpha=0.05, N=11. Power is based on a noncentral-chi-square approximation with lambda = N(k-1)W.

At N=11, the minimum detectable Kendall's W is 0.438 for 80% power and 0.575 for 90% power.
